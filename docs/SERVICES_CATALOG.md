## 3. The 6 Interactive Simulators

Each sector features a custom interactive Blade widget located in `resources/views/partials/simulators/`:

1. **BrandERP Business & ROI Simulator (`brand-erp-simulator.blade.php`):**
   * Computes monthly time saved and direct monetary savings based on monthly invoice count and branch numbers.
2. **AI Agents Conversation Simulator (`ai-agents-simulator.blade.php`):**
   * Live interactive chat simulation demonstrating an Egyptian sales conversation on WhatsApp with lead scoring.
3. **Cybersecurity Scanner & Hardening Benchmark (`cybersecurity-scanner.blade.php`):**
   * Multi-question interactive assessment generating a security score (0-100%) and instant hardening recommendations.
4. **Engineering Maintenance Cost Calculator (`maintenance-benchmark.blade.php`):**
   * Calculates visit duration, labor costs, and parts estimate with live discount toggles.
5. **Business Consulting Diagnostic (`business-consulting-diagnostic.blade.php`):**
   * Enterprise maturity matrix identifying departmental bottlenecks across finance, sales, and supply chain.
6. **Academy Curriculum Explorer (`academy-curriculum-explorer.blade.php`):**
   * Interactive syllabus viewer with week-by-week lesson breakdowns, live labs, and registration links.
